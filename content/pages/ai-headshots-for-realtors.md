## What works best for real estate

**Attire.** Business casual reads as approachable and local. A blazer over an open collar or a simple top in a solid color photographs best. Save the full suit for luxury and commercial listings.

**Background.** Clean studio grey or soft white keeps the attention on your face and works on every brokerage template. The Creative style with a soft, blurred outdoor background suits agents who sell neighborhoods and lifestyle.

**Expression.** Warm and open beats serious. A relaxed, genuine smile is the single biggest factor in whether a stranger reaches out.
